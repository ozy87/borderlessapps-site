Chipwise

Microchip records, reminders, and lost-pet QR cards

What Chipwise does

Chipwise keeps your own record of your pet's microchip number, the database it is registered with, and your contact details in one place. Add a pet, save its chip details, and set reminders so you remember to check that the registration and your contact details are still correct.

Why pet owners use it

  • One place for every pet's chip number and registration database, with no digging through old paperwork or emails
  • Reminders to check your registration, book vaccinations, and review your contact details
  • A shareable lost-pet QR card that shows key details to whoever finds your pet
  • Works for more than one pet

Key features

  • Pet profiles with name, species, breed, date of birth, and photo
  • Microchip number and registration database for each pet
  • Owner contact details attached to each pet
  • Reminders for registration, vaccination, and contact-detail checks
  • Shareable, revocable lost-pet QR card
  • Available in English, Dutch, and Polish

Not a microchip database

Chipwise is not a microchip database and does not register your pet's chip. Keep your details up to date with your microchip database; if you don't know which one, check-a-chip.co.uk can tell you for UK databases.

Your data

Chipwise works without an account. Your records are saved to secure storage in the EU under a random identifier, with chip numbers and contact details stored encrypted, and you can delete everything from within the app at any time. The app is free and shows a banner ad. See the Privacy Policy for full details, including how lost-pet links work.

Independent app

Chipwise is an independent app from Borderless Apps. It is not affiliated with any microchip database, with KROPiK, mObywatel, the Chipnummerportaal, the NVWA, or any government body. Chipwise does not scan microchips (a phone's NFC cannot read ISO 11784/11785 pet chips), and nothing in the app is veterinary advice. Always register your pet with the database required in your country.
