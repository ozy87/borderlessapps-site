Chipwise Support

Need help with Chipwise? Email us at support@borderlessapps.com and we'll respond within 1-2 business days. Please include your device model, OS version, app version, and a short description of the problem so we can help faster.

For your safety, please don't email us your full microchip number, home address, or a lost-pet card link unless it's necessary to resolve your issue.

Chipwise is not a microchip database

Chipwise is not a microchip database and does not register your pet's chip. It is your own record of the details. To register a chip, change your address or phone number, or transfer keepership, contact the database your pet's chip is registered with. If you don't know which one that is, check-a-chip.co.uk can tell you for UK databases. We cannot look up, change, or confirm a registration for you.

Reminders and notifications

If reminders aren't showing up, check that notifications are enabled for Chipwise in your device settings and that a reminder is set for the relevant pet inside the app. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Lost-pet cards

A lost-pet card is a shareable link, also shown as a QR code, that displays your pet's name and chip number and the contact details you saved. You can revoke a card at any time in the app, and links stop working automatically after 90 days. If you think a link was shared without your permission, revoke it straight away and contact us.

Pet photos

To add or change a photo, open the pet, choose Edit, and tap the photo. If Chipwise can't open your photo library, allow photo access for Chipwise in your device settings.

Ads and your ad choices

Chipwise is free and shows a banner ad. In the UK, the EEA, and Switzerland you are asked for your ad consent choices the first time ads load, and you can change them in Settings, under Ads, with "Manage ad privacy choices".

Deleting your data

You can delete all of your data in the app: go to Settings > Your data > Delete all my data. This removes your pets, pet photos, chip details, owner contacts, reminders, and lost-pet cards from our storage and cancels scheduled notifications. Do this before removing the app: there are no accounts, so we cannot find your records for you afterwards.
