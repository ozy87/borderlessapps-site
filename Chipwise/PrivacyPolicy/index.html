Chipwise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

Overview

Chipwise is an app from Borderless Apps for keeping your own record of your pet's microchip details, your contact details, and reminders, and for creating a lost-pet card you can share. The app works without an account. This policy explains what Chipwise stores, where, why, and what you can do about it.

Chipwise is not a microchip database

Chipwise is not a microchip database and does not register your pet's chip. Nothing you enter in Chipwise is passed to any microchip database, registry, vet, or government body. Keep your details up to date with your microchip database; if you don't know which one holds your pet's chip, check-a-chip.co.uk can tell you for UK databases.

Who is responsible for your data

Chipwise is developed and operated by Borderless Apps LLC, which is the data controller for the information described here. You can reach us at privacy@borderlessapps.com for privacy questions and requests, or support@borderlessapps.com for help with the app.

What we store and where

Chipwise stores only what you choose to enter:

  • Pets: name, species, breed, date of birth, and notes.
  • Pet photos: a photo, if you add one.
  • Microchip details: the chip number, the registration database you say it is listed with, the registration date, and the country.
  • Owner contacts: name, phone number, email address, and postal address.
  • Reminders: the type of reminder, how often it repeats, its next date, and whether you opened, snoozed, or dismissed it.
  • Lost-pet cards: a copy of the pet's name, chip number, registration database, country, and the owner name, phone, email, and address, made when you create a card.

These records are always saved to a database hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). There is no setting that keeps them only on your phone. They are stored against a random identifier that the app creates on your device, not against an account, name, or email address. That identifier is kept in your device's secure storage and is sent with each request so the app can find your records.

Encryption. Everything travels between the app and our storage over an encrypted connection. Microchip numbers, owner contact details, and lost-pet card contents are also stored encrypted in the database.

Photos. Pet photos are kept in file storage in the same EU region. Each photo has a web address made of long random identifiers. That address is not listed anywhere and cannot be guessed, but it is not protected by a password, so anyone you gave the exact address to could open the photo.

On your device. Your language choice, the list of scheduled reminders, and whether you have seen the welcome screens are stored only on your device.

Lost-pet cards

When you create a lost-pet card, Chipwise makes a link you can share or print as a QR code. Anyone who opens that link can see the pet's name, chip number, registration database and country, and the owner name, phone, email, and address on the card. Share it only when you need help finding your pet. A link stops working after 90 days, and you can revoke it at any time in the app.

Reminders and notifications

Reminders are scheduled as notifications on your device. Chipwise does not use a push notification service and does not collect a push token. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

Analytics (PostHog)

Chipwise uses PostHog, hosted in the EU, to understand which features are used. It records a small number of events: a pet profile was created, a photo was added, a reminder was acted on, a reminder could not be scheduled, and an ad banner was shown. With each event PostHog receives a random analytics identifier stored on your device, your device model, operating system, app version and language, and the IP address the request came from.

Analytics events never include your pet's name, chip number, registration details, or your contact details, and the analytics identifier is separate from the identifier your records are stored under. Chipwise does not record your screen or collect crash reports.

Advertising (Google AdMob) and the consent form

Chipwise is free and shows a banner ad from Google AdMob on the home and reminders screens.

  • In the UK, the EEA, and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, Google's consent form asks whether you agree to the use of your device's storage and identifiers for advertising and to personalised ads. No ad is requested until you have made a choice. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, under Ads, with "Manage ad privacy choices".
  • Elsewhere: Chipwise asks Google for non-personalised ads only.

To show and measure ads, Google receives your IP address (from which it estimates your approximate location), device identifiers, information about the ads shown and how you interact with them, and diagnostic information. On Android this includes the advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings. On iPhone and iPad, Chipwise does not ask for permission to track you and does not read the advertising identifier. Google handles this data as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Chipwise never sends Google your pet's details, chip number, or contact details.

Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • Your records, photos, reminders, and lost-pet cards: to provide the app you asked for (performance of a contract).
  • Analytics: our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app, using pseudonymous data that is never linked to your name, contact details or pet records.
  • Advertising: your consent, where the consent form applies; otherwise our legitimate interest in funding a free app with non-personalised ads.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase: database and file storage, in the EU (Ireland).
  • PostHog: analytics, in the EU.
  • Google AdMob: advertising and the consent form. Google may process data outside the UK and EEA under its own safeguards.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share your records with anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything yourself in the app: go to Settings > Your data > Delete all my data. This permanently removes your pets, pet photos, microchip details, owner contacts, reminders, and lost-pet cards from our storage, cancels scheduled notifications, and clears the app's saved preferences on your device. Lost-pet links stop working immediately. You can also delete a single pet, which removes its photo, chip details, contacts, reminders, and lost-pet cards.

Deleting your data in the app does not remove the random analytics identifier on your device or the ad consent choice held by Google. Removing the app clears both.

Delete before you remove the app. Because there are no accounts, we cannot tell which records are yours once the app and its identifier are gone, and we cannot search by name or email because contact details are stored encrypted.

How long we keep data

  • Your records and photos: until you delete them in the app.
  • Lost-pet cards: the link works for 90 days or until you revoke it. The card itself is removed when you delete the pet or all your data.
  • Analytics events: for the retention period of our PostHog project, after which PostHog deletes them.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own retention rules.

Your rights under UK and EU data protection law

If you are in the UK or the EU, you have the right to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including analytics;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent to personalised advertising at any time.

How to use these rights. Everything Chipwise holds about you and your pets is visible in the app, where you can correct or delete it straight away. You can change your advertising choice in Settings, under Ads. For anything else, email privacy@borderlessapps.com and we will reply within one month. Because records are not linked to a name or email address, we may need you to show us the request comes from the device that holds them before we can act on it.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority: the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands, the UODO in Poland, or the authority in your own EU country.

Children

Chipwise is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above.
