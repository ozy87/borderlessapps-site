WireWise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

WireWise is an electrician's calculator and reference app from Borderless Apps. It works without an account and does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or photos.

Information Stored on Your Device

Your calculations, saved values and app settings stay on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Analytics

From version 1.0.1, WireWise uses PostHog (servers in the European Union) for anonymous product analytics: for example, when the app is opened and which calculators and features are used. These events are sent with a random install identifier, the app version, the device model and the operating system version. We do not link this information to your identity, sell it, or use it for advertising.

Purchases

WireWise is sold through the Apple App Store. Apple handles payment, and we do not receive your payment details.

Third-Party Services

Apple (App Store) and PostHog (analytics) process data as described above, under their own privacy policies.

Children

WireWise is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Contact us at privacy@borderlessapps.com.
