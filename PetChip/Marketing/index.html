PetChip

Microchip records, reminders, and lost-pet QR cards

What PetChip does

PetChip is a companion app that keeps your pet's microchip details, registration information, and owner contact details in one place on your phone. Add a pet profile, save the microchip number and which registration database it's listed with, and set reminders so registration and contact details never go stale.

Why pet owners use it

  • One place for every pet's chip number and registration database — no digging through old paperwork or emails
  • Reminders for registration renewals, vaccinations, and contact-detail checks
  • A shareable lost-pet QR card that shows key details to whoever finds your pet
  • Works for multiple pets and multiple countries

Key features

  • Pet profiles with name, species, breed, date of birth, and photo
  • Microchip number and registration database tracking
  • Owner contact details attached to each pet
  • Reminders for registration, vaccination, and contact-detail checks
  • Shareable, revocable lost-pet QR card
  • Available in English, Dutch, and Polish

Privacy-conscious by design

PetChip works without creating an account. Your pet and contact details are stored using an anonymous, device-scoped identifier, and you can delete everything from within the app at any time. See the Privacy Policy for full details, including how lost-pet QR links work.

Independent app disclaimer

PetChip is an independent app from Borderless Apps and is not an official microchip registry. It is not affiliated with KROPiK, mObywatel, Chipnummerportaal, NVWA, DEFRA-approved databases, or any government body. PetChip does not scan microchips — phone NFC cannot read ISO 11784/11785 pet chips — and nothing in the app is veterinary advice. Always register your pet with the official database required in your country.
