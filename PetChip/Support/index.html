PetChip Support

Need help with PetChip? Email us at support@borderlessapps.com and we'll respond within 1-2 business days. Please include your device model, OS version, app version, and a short description of the problem so we can help faster.

For your safety, please don't email us your full microchip number, home address, or a lost-pet card link unless it's necessary to resolve your issue — support tickets aren't the place to store that information.

Reminders and notifications

If reminders aren't showing up, check that notifications are enabled for PetChip in your device settings and that reminders are turned on for the relevant pet inside the app. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Lost-pet cards

Lost-pet QR cards are shareable links that show the details you chose to include, such as your pet's chip number and your contact information. You can revoke a card at any time from within the app, and cards expire automatically after 90 days. If you believe a lost-pet link was shared without your permission, revoke it immediately and contact us.

Data deletion

You can delete all of your data directly in the app: go to Settings > Your data > Delete all my data. This removes your pets, chip records, owner contacts, reminders, lost-pet cards, and scheduled notifications from your device and its sync storage.
