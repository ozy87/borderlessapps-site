PetChip Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 July 2026

Overview

PetChip is a pet microchip records companion from Borderless Apps. The app works without an account, and this policy explains what information PetChip stores and why.

Who operates the app

PetChip is developed and operated by Borderless Apps. You can reach us at support@borderlessapps.com or privacy@borderlessapps.com.

Information you enter

PetChip stores the information you choose to enter for each pet, which may include: pet name, species, breed, date of birth, a photo reference, and notes; microchip number, registration database, registration date, and country; owner name, phone, email, and address; reminder settings; language and app preferences; and lost-pet card data. You control what you add, and none of it is required to use the app beyond what a feature needs.

App storage and sync

PetChip uses Supabase to store your app data under an anonymous, device-scoped identifier rather than an account or email address. Microchip numbers, owner contact details, and lost-pet card records are handled through the app's encrypted storage architecture. Local preferences (such as language and settings) are stored on your device with AsyncStorage, and your anonymous device identifier is stored with SecureStore.

Lost-pet QR cards

When you create a lost-pet QR card, PetChip generates a shareable, revocable link. Anyone who opens that link can see the pet name, chip number, registration database and country, and the owner name, phone, email, and address you included on the card — so only share it when you need help finding your pet. Lost-pet links expire automatically after 90 days, and you can revoke a link at any time from within the app.

Notifications

PetChip uses Expo Notifications to send optional local reminders, such as registration renewals, vaccination checks, and contact-detail reviews. These reminders are scheduled on your device and can be turned off in the app's settings.

Analytics and ads

PetChip is free and ad-supported, using Google AdMob to show banner ads. Ads default to non-personalized unless you opt in to personalized ads consent. If configured, PetChip may also use PostHog to record anonymous usage and diagnostic events (such as which features are used or whether the app crashed) — these events do not include your pet's microchip number, registration details, or owner contact information.

Third-party services

PetChip may use the following services to operate the app:

  • Supabase for anonymous, device-scoped app storage.
  • Google AdMob for banner ads.
  • Expo Notifications for local reminder scheduling.
  • PostHog for privacy-conscious usage and diagnostic analytics, if enabled.

Data deletion

You can delete your data at any time from within the app: go to Settings > Your data > Delete all my data. This removes your pets, chip records, owner contacts, reminders, lost-pet cards, scheduled notifications, and local preferences for that device and its associated sync storage.

Children

PetChip is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests? Contact us at privacy@borderlessapps.com.
