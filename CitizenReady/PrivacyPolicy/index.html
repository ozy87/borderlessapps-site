CitizenReady Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 July 2026

Overview

CitizenReady is a Life in the UK test practice trainer from Borderless Apps. The app works without an account and does not ask for personal details.

Information Processed by the App

CitizenReady processes your chapter practice answers, mock exam results, and app settings (such as dark mode) so it can show your progress and result history. The app does not request your name, email address, location, camera, or photo library access.

Local Storage

Mock exam results, wrong-answer review history, chapter progress, and app settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes this local data from your device.

Purchases

The one-time premium unlock is processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play and managed using RevenueCat, which verifies your purchase receipt to activate premium access. RevenueCat may process an anonymous device identifier and purchase/receipt data for this purpose. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

Analytics

CitizenReady may use PostHog for privacy-conscious, anonymous product analytics, such as which features are used. Analytics events do not include your name, email address, or other directly identifying details.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests? Contact us at privacy@borderlessapps.com.
