CitizenReady

CitizenReady helps you practise for the Life in the UK test. Work through chapter-by-chapter practice sessions or take a timed 24-question mock exam that mirrors the real test format, then review every wrong answer with a clear explanation.

  • Offline by default — no connection needed, ever.
  • One payment, everything included — a one-time unlock, no subscription.
  • Human-reviewed content, checked against the official handbook.
  • Full result history saved locally on your device.

CitizenReady is an unofficial, independent practice trainer from Borderless Apps. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the UK Government, Home Office, UKVI, TSO (The Stationery Office), or PSI Services. It does not book your test. For official test booking, visit gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test.
