RepairScore Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 June 2026

Overview

RepairScore is an EU product repairability scanner from Borderless Apps. We design the app to work without an account and without asking for personal details.

Information Processed by the App

RepairScore may process product barcodes, brand/model searches, repairability lookup results, app settings, and local scan history so the app can show repairability information and cached results.

If you use the repair locator, the app may request your device location to show nearby repair options. Location access is optional and is used only for that feature.

Local Storage

Recent scans, cached product results, language settings, and disclaimer status may be stored on your device to make the app faster and available offline. Deleting the app removes this local data from your device.

Third-Party Services

RepairScore may use the following services to operate the app:

  • EU EPREL registry for public product repairability data.
  • Supabase for product cache and repair-shop directory data.
  • PostHog for privacy-conscious analytics, if enabled.
  • Google AdMob for ads, if ads are shown.
  • Apple Maps, Google Maps, or the device maps app when you open directions.

Analytics and Ads

Analytics are used to understand whether core features work reliably. We do not require an account, and analytics events should not include your name, email address, or other directly identifying details. Ads may be served by Google AdMob according to Google's policies.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests? Contact us at privacy@borderlessapps.com.
